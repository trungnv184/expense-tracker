## Decisions
- Performance is measured on a full reload ("opening the app"), because seeding writes straight to IndexedDB and live queries only pick that up after a reload.

## Follow-ups / known limitations
- The save timing (≤ 150 ms against a 200 ms budget) has little headroom on slow CI machines.
- The perf save test waits for the new row but doesn't assert the month total. The verifier's probe confirmed the total updates (+$1.23 in 78 ms).
- There's a stray comment in a11y.spec.ts about the focus walk.
