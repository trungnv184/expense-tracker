## What was done
- `SummaryPage`: month navigation, the total, a legend with all 5 categories (each linking to the filtered list) and percentages, a donut chart, a budget column, and an empty state.
- `DonutChart`: hand-written SVG ring with `role="img"` and a descriptive label. Only categories with spending get a segment.
- `BudgetBar`: "$X left" or "$X over" written out, a progress bar (capped at 100%) in ok/warn/over state, or "No budget".
- An e2e test checks that editing an expense moves its amount between category totals (follow-up from M2-G3).

## Decisions
- The Summary h1 is the month name, as in the mockup. The route tests were updated, and the AppShell test now waits for the async heading.
- Only the legend entries link to the filtered list; budget rows are not links. This avoids nesting a "Set a budget" link inside another link.
