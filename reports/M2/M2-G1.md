## What was done
- `ExpenseFormPage` (add mode) with amount, category, date (defaults to today) and note (≤ 200 characters). Errors appear inline, focus moves to the first invalid field, and if saving fails a banner appears and the form keeps what was typed.
- Components: `CategoryPicker` (native radio group), `CategoryBadge` and `AmountInput` (shows the currency symbol from settings).
- Hooks: `useCurrency` (live value from settings) and `ToastProvider`/`useToast` inside AppShell.
- e2e fixture `e2e/fixtures/db.ts` to read and seed the app's IndexedDB.
- Tightened `guard()`: our NotFoundError is now recognised by a marker, so a native IndexedDB NotFoundError counts as a storage failure (follow-up from M1-G3).

## Decisions
- After saving, the app goes to `/?month=<month of the expense>`, so an expense added for a past month lands on that month.
- The amount hint says "no thousands separators" (follow-up from M1-G2).
