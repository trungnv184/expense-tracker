## What was done
- `src/domain/recurring.ts`: `dueOccurrences` lists the months after the cursor (or from the start month) up to the current month. The day is clamped to the month's length, and an occurrence isn't due until its day arrives.
- `src/data/recurringRepo.ts`: list, get, create, update (future months only), stop, and `generateDue`, which runs as one read-write transaction over recurring and expenses (bulkAdd plus a cursor update).
